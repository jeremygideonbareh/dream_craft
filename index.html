Our story

Small things, made with attention.

Dream Craft began at a kitchen table in Shillong with one wedding invitation and a very sharp pair of scissors. Today the studio makes stationery, florals and keepsakes for celebrations across India and beyond — still cut, folded, wired and packed by the same pair of hands.
